**smprintinst**(_inst_)
> Print the number, device, and device name for a list of instruments. _inst_ can be a vector of instrument numbers, or an instrument name. If _inst_ is omitted, all instruments are printed.


```
smprintinst
Inst  Device      Dev. Name
------------------------------------------------------------
1  test                 
2  DecaDAC     DAC1     
3  DecaDAC     DAC2     
4  SR830       LockinUpper
5  SR830       LockinLower
5  HP34401A    DMM      
7  AMI430      AMI430   
8  Aux                  
9  AWG5000     AWG1     
10  HP1000A     N9310A   
11  N5183       N5183    
```
